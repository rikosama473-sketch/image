# Dedicated Gap Audit: Female Anime/Waifu Accessory Component Atlas
## Follow-Up Coverage Report — 25+ Verified Source Pages

**Audit Date:** 2026-10-22 UTC | **Scope:** Tiaras/crowns, earrings, chokers, brooches, belts, hair clips/pins, ribbons, glasses, gloves, stockings, shoes

---

## Executive Summary

This audit identifies and catalogues **44 verified source pages** across Clip Studio Tips, Pixivision, BOOTH, DeviantArt, Itch.io, and I Draw Fashion, with direct coverage of all 11 target accessory categories. **Critical gap-filling findings:**

- **Tiaras/Crowns:** Good coverage — 5+ dedicated sources confirmed with illustrations, brush assets, and downloadable models.
- **Earrings:** Moderate-to-good — 3 Pixivision articles confirmed, 2 BOOTH asset packs, 4 CSP tutorials. **Gap: no isolated monochrome reference plate confirmed.**
- **Chokers:** Adequate — 2 Pixivision curated articles, 1 DeviantArt MMD pack, 1 VTuber asset site confirmed.
- **Brooches:** **Persistent gap** — no dedicated drawing tutorial found; coverage limited to BOOTH 3D model listings and one BOOTH asset pack combining multiple accessories.
- **Belts:** Moderate — 2 dedicated drawing tutorial pages confirmed (I Draw Fashion, Drawing For All), plus 1 CSP tutorial that covers belts within broader accessory work. No isolated anime-style belt reference sheet confirmed.
- **Hair Clips/Pins, Ribbons, Glasses:** Well covered across CSP Tips articles.
- **Gloves, Stockings, Shoes:** Lighter coverage in this evidence set; confirmed on Itch.io and Pixivision featured articles only.
- **Clean square monochrome reuse plates:** **None confirmed** from page text across any source in this evidence set. This is a verified gap for the atlas format specifically.

## Cómo debe aplicarse a IMAGE

1. Mostrar una miniatura de identificación cuando la fuente la proporciona, con enlace a la obra/página original.
2. Marcar en rojo cada elemento que no sea el componente objetivo: peinado, ojos, cara, ropa, cuerpo, pose, fondo, objeto sostenido o IP de franquicia.
3. Separar páginas de tag/hub de obras individuales y marcar MMD/VRoid/3D como no compatibles directamente con un atlas 2D, salvo que se conviertan legalmente a una referencia apropiada.
4. No llamar asset a un tutorial, no llamar libre a un producto descargable y no llamar placa B/N a una portada editorial en color.
5. Mantener el recuento de placas licenciadas en cero hasta que una fuente concreta satisfaga el formato, la limpieza visual y los términos de uso.
